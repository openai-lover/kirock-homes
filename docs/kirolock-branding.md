# 킬록 홈즈 · Kirolock Homes

Historical naming record: the current English name is **Kirock Homes**. See [the current design](kirock-design.md). The original text below is preserved as the decision recorded at that stage.

Branding decision recorded on 2026-10-31. This document records a naming and visual-design choice, not a new Kiro development session or a language-market study.

## Localized names

| Use | Name |
| --- | --- |
| Korean display title | **킬록 홈즈** |
| English display title | **Kirolock Homes** |
| Shared subtitle | **Room of Suspects** |
| Existing Korean descriptive subtitle | **범인은 내 방에** |

The Korean title is a localized display name rather than a literal letter-by-letter transliteration. The English spelling preserves **Kiro** visibly and combines it with the detective association of **Sherlock**. **Homes** deliberately echoes the sound of **Holmes** while pointing to homes and the player's own room. This is the intended wordplay; it has not been validated with English-speaking players.

한글은 **킬록 홈즈**, 영어는 **Kirolock Homes**를 사용합니다. Kiro와 Sherlock을 합친 탐정 이름이며, Holmes와 소리가 같은 Homes로 ‘우리 집’ 사진에서 벌어지는 사건이라는 뜻을 겹쳤습니다. **Room of Suspects**는 기존 게임의 부제로 유지합니다.

## Pronunciation and interpretation limits

Kiro's official About page says its name “rhymes with 'hero'.” That describes **Kiro**, not an official pronunciation for the newly coined **Kirolock Homes**. [Official Kiro About](https://kiro.dev/about/), checked 2026-10-31.

The English spelling **Killock** is avoided because its opening letters could suggest **kill**, which may push the name toward a harsher tone. That is a design inference, not a finding from audience research. No actual US, UK or other English-speaking participant survey, pronunciation test or name-recognition study was conducted.

Kiro의 공식 발음 안내는 hero와 운이 맞는다는 설명입니다. 새 이름 전체의 공식 발음을 확인한 것은 아닙니다. Killock의 kill 연상이 거칠게 느껴질 수 있다는 판단은 추정이며, 실제 영미권 사용자 조사를 했다고 주장하지 않습니다.

## Visual direction

Keep the existing deep teal and cream desk palette, warm brass/brown accents, **Jua** display lettering and **Gaegu** handwritten notes. The new guide is a small white ghost detective in a brown cap/cape with a muted teal scarf, notebook and ordinary magnifying glass. Its two simple black oval eyes retain a soft, toy-like appearance; the magnifying glass has no eye inside it.

The player's photograph and three animated object suspects remain the center of the game. The guide character supports the detective theme without replacing the photograph, adding another suspect, or changing the three-statement rules.

기존 청록·크림색과 Jua/Gaegu 글꼴을 이어가며, 새 유령 탐정은 안내 캐릭터로 사용합니다. 실제 사진 속 세 용의자와 작은 SVG 얼굴은 유지합니다.
